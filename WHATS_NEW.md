## 🎁 The Surprises

### 1. 💌 **Hidden Love Letters**
- **What it is**: Floating hearts scattered across the screen
- **How it works**: Click each heart to reveal a secret love letter
- **Special effects**: 
  - Confetti explosion when discovering new letters
  - Progress tracker (Found: X / 4)
  - Completion message when all are found
- **Location in site**: After "Why I Love You" section

**What Lipika will see:**
- Beautiful floating hearts with pulse animations
- Each heart opens a modal with a romantic message
- Checkmarks appear on discovered letters
- Special celebration when she finds all 4

### 2. 🔒 **Secret Photos**
- **What it is**: Locked/blurred mystery photos
- **How it works**: Click to unlock and reveal the real photo
- **Special effects**:
  - Dual confetti bursts from both sides
  - Smooth unlock animation
  - Modal view with romantic caption
  - Progress tracker
- **Location in site**: After regular Gallery section

**What Lipika will see:**
- 4 blurred photos with lock icons
- Click to reveal the real image
- Each photo has a hidden romantic message
- Completion message when all are unlocked

---

### 3. 🎯 **Love Quiz**
- **What it is**: Interactive quiz about your relationship
- **How it works**: 5 multiple-choice questions
- **Special effects**:
  - Confetti on correct answers
  - Instant feedback (green for correct, red for wrong)
  - Fun facts after each question
  - Personalized results based on score
- **Location in site**: After Secret Photos, before Music Player

**What Lipika will see:**
- Start screen with "Our Love Story Quiz"
- 5 questions about your journey
- Progress bar and score tracker
- Beautiful results screen with personalized message
- Option to replay
